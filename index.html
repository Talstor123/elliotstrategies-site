FAQ

Straight answers.

Don't see your question? Just ask.

How much does it cost?

Every situation is different, so pricing is case by case. Services start at $3,000, and we take a 75% deposit to get started.

When is the rest due?

The remaining 25% is due when the job is done.

How do I pay?

Card, crypto or invoice.

What if you can't fix it?

We don't guarantee results, and nobody honest can. But if we can't fix the problem, you get at least 80% of your money back, and often all of it. The small amount we may keep only covers work already done. (Refunds don't apply to proactive PR and image-building work.)

Can you really get things removed?

Most things, yes, including content other firms say can't be touched. We have working relationships at news outlets and social media platforms, and methods we've built over years. We'll tell you straight what's realistic for your situation before we start.

Can you remove mugshots and arrest records?

Yes.

Can you remove reviews?

No. Review removal on Google, Yelp and similar sites isn't something we offer.

Can you find out who's behind a fake account or an attack?

Yes. We use open-source intelligence and investigative methods to track down who's behind it.

How fast will I see results?

It depends on the case. Some takedowns happen fast; search clean-up and bigger situations take longer. You get a written timeline before any work starts.

Will the content come back after it's removed?

If you add monitoring, we keep watching and deal with anything that resurfaces. Without it, the job ends once the content is down.

Do you offer ongoing or monthly work?

Yes. Monitoring and ongoing reputation upkeep are available, priced on the hours and what you need.

Will anyone know I hired you?

No. Every client signs an NDA with us, and confidentiality is the whole business. If clients couldn't trust us to stay quiet, we wouldn't have clients.

Do you work with businesses too?

Yes. We work with companies of all sizes, as well as individuals.

Do you work with people outside the US?

Yes. We work with clients anywhere.

Can someone reach out for me?

Yes. Agents, managers, lawyers, publicists and campaign staff reach out on behalf of clients all the time.

Do you work with lawyers?

Yes. When a case needs legal action, we work with outside attorneys. We don't have in-house lawyers.

What do you need from me to get started?

Tell us what's going on and send any links or screenshots you have. If there's a legal case involved, we'll need most, if not all, of the files related to it.

Can you help if nothing's wrong yet?

Yes. We run positive PR campaigns and build up your online presence so you look good before a launch, campaign or big moment. Refunds don't apply to this kind of work.

Is there anything you won't take on?

We don't take cases involving sex crimes, whether it's a conviction or allegations where the evidence points to guilt. Everything else, we'll look at.

Who's behind Elliot Strategies?

Elliot Strategies is run personally by its founder, who has a background in government crisis communications and public relations, plus OSINT, HUMINT and investigations. You work with him directly from start to finish. Your case never gets handed off to someone junior.
