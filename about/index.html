Hi, I'm Jake.

I started Elliot Strategies because I've seen how fast one story, one post or one search result can undo something a person spent years building.

No judgment. Just results.

Most people don't think about their reputation until something goes wrong. Then it's all they can think about. A headline gets passed around, an old mugshot shows up on the first page of Google, a fake account starts posting in your name, and suddenly that's the first thing anyone knows about you.

I've spent my career around situations where things were going wrong: in emergency services, in security, and in government crisis communications. What I took from all of it is that the people who come out okay are the ones who get the right help early, stay calm and work a real plan.

It's not my job to judge you, and I'm not going to. Whatever happened, however it happened, I'm not here to decide whether you deserve help or make you explain yourself. You don't owe me a good story. I just need the facts, and then I get to work fixing it.

I'm selective about the cases I take, and I keep the list small on purpose. When I take one on, I treat it like it's my own name on the line.

Background

My background doesn't fit neatly in one box, and that turned out to be the point. Fixing a reputation means finding out what's really out there, figuring out who's behind it, deciding what to say, and then doing the work of cleaning it up. I've done each of those things in some form for years.

Emergency servicesYears of showing up when things go wrong. You learn to stay calm, think clearly and act fast.

Put together, it means you're not getting handed off between an investigator, a PR person and a marketing agency. It's one person who understands the whole picture.
